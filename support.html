Frequently Asked Questions

Does SilvaCruise work offline?

Yes. SilvaCruise works 100% offline with no cell service required. All data is stored locally on your device.

What volume equations does SilvaCruise use?

SilvaCruise uses published, peer-reviewed volume equations: Wensel & Olsen (1995) for 8 major western conifers, Clark stem profile equations (SE-282) for 49 southern species, and Form Class 78 tables as a fallback for all other species.

What cruise methods are supported?

Variable radius (point sampling / prism), fixed radius plots, tree-based tally, and 100% measure. Quick BA cruises are also available for fast reconnaissance.

Can I export my cruise data?

Yes. Cruise reports can be exported to CSV and XLSX formats for use in spreadsheets and other software.

What log rules are supported?

Scribner Decimal C, International 1/4", and Doyle.

Is my data backed up?

All cruise data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, access, or store your data on any server. Please back up your device regularly to avoid data loss.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email us at silvacruiseapp@gmail.com with a description of the issue or your feature idea. Screenshots are helpful for bug reports.
